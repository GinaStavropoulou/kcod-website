On the 14<sup>th</sup> of May 2019, Lorenzo presented at the
first [International
Workshop on the Legal Notions of Privacy and Data Protection in EU Law in a
Rapidly Changing World](https://brusselsprivacyhub.eu/events/article%207%208%20workshop.html), held by the Brussels Privacy Hub and by the Law,
Science, Technology and Society (LSTS) department at VUB. The reception of his
latest paper (“A Right to a Rule – on the substance and essence of the
fundamental right to personal data protection”) has been very positive. The
paper has been submitted for publication in an edited volume by Hart
Publishing.

On the 15<sup>th</sup> of May, Lorenzo presented his research (i.e. the “Right to a Rule” paper as embedded in the broader context of his doctoral thesis) at [TILTing Perspectives 2019](https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/research/institutes-and-research-groups/tilt/events/tilting-perspectives) (“Regulating a World in Transition”) too. The reception was very positive there as well. An abstract of the paper is available [here](https://easychair.org/smart-program/TILTing2019/2019-05-15.html#talk:89331).
