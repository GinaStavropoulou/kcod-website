<p>On the 14<sup>th</sup> of May 2019, Lorenzo presented at the
first <a href="https://brusselsprivacyhub.eu/events/article%207%208%20workshop.html">International
Workshop on the Legal Notions of Privacy and Data Protection in EU Law in a
Rapidly Changing World</a>, held by the Brussels Privacy Hub and by the Law,
Science, Technology and Society (LSTS) department at VUB. The reception of his
latest paper (“A Right to a Rule – on the substance and essence of the
fundamental right to personal data protection”) has been very positive. The
paper has been submitted for publication in an edited volume by Hart
Publishing. </p>
<p>On the 15<sup>th</sup> of May, Lorenzo presented his research (i.e. the “Right to a Rule” paper as embedded in the broader context of his doctoral thesis) at <a href="https://www.tilburguniversity.edu/research/institutes-and-research-groups/tilt/events/tilting-perspectives">TILTing Perspectives 2019</a> (“Regulating a World in Transition”) too. The reception was very positive there as well. An abstract of the paper is available <a href="https://easychair.org/smart-program/TILTing2019/2019-05-15.html#talk:89331">here</a>.</p>
